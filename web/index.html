STUDY SCOPE

Interpretation
and limitations.

The task is prediction of a supplied dataset label, not prospective risk estimation. Dataset provenance, access terms, measurement units, patient/group identity, and row independence have not been independently confirmed. External validation was not performed.

The holdout results describe one partition of this dataset and were not used for further model or threshold selection. The separate unlabeled test.csv was not used to calculate performance metrics.
